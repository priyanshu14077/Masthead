01 · Logo

A nameplate and its rule.

The wordmark is Newsreader Semibold in capitals, tracked wide, over a thick copper rule and a thin ink one. The mark keeps the M and the rule for small spaces. Both are outlined paths, so they need no fonts.

xx

Clear space

Keep a margin of x on every side, where x is the cap height of the letters. Around the mark, keep a quarter of its width. Nothing else enters that box: no text, no edges, no other logos.

Minimum size

Wordmark: 96px wide on screen, 25mm in print. Mark: 24px or 6mm. Below 24px, use the favicon, which drops the thin rule so the M stays legible.

04 · Voice and tone

Write like an editor who has read the code.

Concrete over clever

Name the system, the file, the number. If a sentence fits any product, cut it.

DoThe post arrives as a pull request on your blog's repo.

Don'tSeamlessly supercharge your content pipeline.

Short sentences

One idea per sentence. Most sentences end sooner than you expect.

DoPick a date. The post goes out when it arrives.

Don'tOur intelligent scheduling engine empowers teams to orchestrate publishing at the perfect moment.

Only claims we can prove

No invented customers, counts, ratings or awards. If proof doesn't exist yet, leave the space empty.

DoMasthead never marks up model usage.

Don'tTrusted by 500+ DevRel teams worldwide.

Honest about limits

Engineers trust a tool that says where it stops.

DoThe check can miss things. It catches the common slips early.

Don'tNever worry about leaks again.

Talk about the product, not the page

No directions to the reader and no lines that repeat a heading.

DoSeven steps. One post. One pull request.

Don'tScroll down to see how it works!

British spelling, no em dashes

Colour, organise, licence. Use a full stop or a comma where a dash wants to go.

DoThe colour palette has one accent.

Don'tThe color palette — bold and revolutionary.

Pricing questions

On the bill.

What do we pay Anthropic?

Whatever your own usage costs on your Anthropic account, at Anthropic's rates. Masthead adds nothing to it.

How does annual billing work?

You pay for 10 months and get 12. The seat minimums are the same as monthly.

Why do Team and Business have a seat minimum?

Both plans are built around review, which takes at least a writer and a reviewer. Team starts at 2 seats and Business at 5.

Is Free a trial?

No. Free is a plan for one writer and one blog, with outlines, drafts, the confidentiality check, thumbnails and pull requests.

Which plan has single sign-on?

Business. It also adds approval rules, an audit log and priority support.
